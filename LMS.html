Dear LMS users, please ignore the username and password field above, If you would:

  1. Log in via the "IPB Central Authentication Service" button instead. You will be automatically redirected to the Central Authentication Service login page. It would automatically accept you without any redirection if you have logged in on some other University websites.

  3. Type in your account password and double-check your typed password like its uppercase, lowercase, number, and alphanumeric digits.
  4. Click the login button and you will be redirected back to this LMS page.
